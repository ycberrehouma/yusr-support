Yusr Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Yusr is designed to provide a private and focused Quran reading, listening, reflection, and spiritual companion experience.

This policy covers the Yusr – Quran Companion app. It also covers Yusr Social Publisher, the private tool used to publish Yusr's own videos to social media; see Yusr Social Publisher below.

Information Stored on Your Device

Yusr stores most app information locally on your device. This may include:

  • Quran reading progress and reading goals
  • Bookmarks and saved reading positions
  • Listening history and playback progress
  • Selected reciters and audio preferences
  • Guided routine progress and completion history
  • Dhikr activity and preferences
  • Personal Moments created inside the app
  • App language, appearance, text size, and translation preferences
  • Reminder preferences
  • Widget preferences
  • Journey progress and achievements

This information is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to a Yusr user account or Yusr-operated server.

Accounts

Yusr does not currently require users to create an account or sign in.

Analytics and Advertising

Yusr does not use advertising networks, behavioral advertising, or third-party analytics services to track how you use the app.

Yusr does not sell your personal information.

Crash and Diagnostic Data

Technical crash information may be processed by services used to distribute and maintain the app. This information is used for app functionality, reliability, troubleshooting, and improving the stability of Yusr.

Crash information is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Quran Audio

Yusr may stream or download Quran recitations from external audio providers. When your device connects to these services, standard internet information such as your IP address and the requested audio file may be visible to those providers as part of normal internet communication.

Yusr does not intentionally send your reading history, bookmarks, personal Moments, guided routine history, or other locally stored personal activity to these audio providers.

Notifications

Yusr may provide local notifications and reminders if you enable them. Reminder preferences and scheduled notifications are managed on your device.

Lock Screen Widget

Yusr may provide Quran verses through an iOS widget. Widget preferences are stored locally on your device.

Children's Privacy

Yusr is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not currently require an account or request personal identifying information as part of normal use.

Third-Party Services

Yusr relies on certain third-party infrastructure for functionality such as application delivery, updates, and Quran audio content. These services may process technical information required to provide their services.

Yusr Social Publisher

Yusr Social Publisher is a private tool that the Yusr developer runs on their own computer to publish Yusr's own promotional videos to Yusr's official social media accounts. It is not offered to the public, and it is not used to publish on behalf of anyone else. Its use is governed by the Yusr Social Publisher Terms of Service.

Platforms and authorization

The Publisher currently connects to YouTube (through Google's OAuth sign-in) and to Yusr's Facebook Page and Instagram account (through Meta's Graph API). Yusr intends to connect its own TikTok account through TikTok's OAuth authorization. Each connection is authorized by the owner of the Yusr account on that platform, and only Yusr's own accounts are connected.

Access tokens

The access tokens and refresh tokens issued by these platforms are stored in local files on the computer that runs the Publisher. They are not sent to a Yusr server or database, and they are used only to call the platform's own interfaces to publish videos and to check whether a post was published.

Videos and captions

Videos are read from a folder on that same computer and uploaded directly to the selected platform, together with the title, description or caption written for that platform. Once uploaded, the video and its text are handled by the platform under its own terms and privacy policy.

Publishing records

To avoid publishing the same video twice, the Publisher keeps a local record for each video: its file name, a fingerprint (SHA-256 hash) of the file, and, for each platform, the post ID, post link, publishing status, times of attempts, and any error message returned by the platform.

What the Publisher does not do

The Publisher does not collect or store information about viewers, followers, or other users of these platforms, and it does not read their comments or messages. It does not use analytics or advertising services. It does not collect any information from people who use the Yusr app.

Revoking access and deletion

Access for any platform can be revoked at any time from that platform's account or connected-apps settings. The Publisher does not delete its local files automatically; tokens and publishing records remain on the operator's computer until they are removed. To request deletion of data associated with the Publisher, see Data Deletion or contact contact@yusrquran.com.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Yusr's features or privacy practices change. The updated version will be published on this page.
