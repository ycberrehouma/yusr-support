Data Deletion – Yusr Social Publisher

Last updated: November 12, 2026

About Yusr Social Publisher

Yusr Social Publisher is a private tool used by the Yusr team to publish content to Yusr's official Facebook and Instagram accounts. It is not a public app and is not offered for use by the general public.

Requesting Data Deletion

If you would like us to delete data associated with you in connection with Yusr Social Publisher, please send an email to:

Email: yusrquranapp@gmail.com

Subject: Yusr Social Publisher – Data Deletion Request

Please include enough information for us to identify the data your request relates to, such as the Facebook or Instagram account involved. We may contact you to verify your request before acting on it.

What Happens After Your Request

Once your request has been verified, applicable data under our control that is associated with Yusr Social Publisher will be deleted, subject to any legal requirements to retain certain information.

Removing App Access in Meta

You can also revoke Yusr Social Publisher's access to your Facebook or Instagram account at any time through your Meta account settings, under the apps and websites connected to your account. Removing access there stops the app from accessing your account going forward.
